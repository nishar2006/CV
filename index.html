01

BPM Transition Project Management

RevOpsCustom ObjectsProject ManagementWorkflow AutomationHubSpot

Replaced fragmented Excel / Smartsheet tracking with a HubSpot-based transition management system from Deal Won through operational handover.

Business problem

Manual spreadsheet tracking limited portfolio visibility, accountability, escalation visibility, and the ability to manage large numbers of procedures consistently.

Solution architecture

  • Three custom objects: Transition Project, Transition Planning Progress, and Procedure.
  • Five workflow levels manage project initialization, planning status, procedure lifecycle, date synchronization, and cross-record data synchronization.
  • Procedure lifecycle spans Capture & SOP, SOP Approved, POC, KPI & QC Approved, and SOP Uploaded & Go Live.
  • Management views cover project health, planning, procedures, resources, and original/current/actual schedules.
TechnologyHubSpot Custom Objects · 26 Workflows · Date Automation · Cross-Record Sync · Dashboards
Business impactCreates a structured HubSpot operating system for transition delivery with standardized lifecycle, schedule visibility, accountability, and management intervention.
02

Customer Service Escalation Management System

BPMHubSpotService HubTicketingSLAAutomationRCA

Centralized the BPM Department's fragmented escalation process in HubSpot, creating a unified ticketing framework for intake, client/LOB routing, ownership, SLA monitoring, escalation, RCA, closure control, and management visibility across multiple Lines of Business.

Business problem

Within the BPM Department, different Lines of Business handled client escalations through fragmented processes. Direct emails to individual owners and missing client associations could cause tickets to be missed or delayed, while leadership lacked a consistent organization-level view of escalation volume, ownership, SLA performance, resolution progress, and accountability.

HubSpot solution architecture

  • A centralized email ID feeds escalations into HubSpot Inbox and a unified BPM Support ticket pipeline.
  • Ticket, contact, and company associations are validated before routing; tickets without reliable client identification are sent to the front desk for manual association.
  • Client and LOB logic assigns the appropriate primary owner, secondary owner, portfolio manager, and DOM/OM through predefined ownership mappings.
  • Supporting workflows handle SLA alerts, escalation, closed-ticket responses, and RCA routing into PWM (Performance Work Management).
TechnologyHubSpot Service Hub · HubSpot Inbox · BPM Support Ticket Pipeline · Company / Contact Associations · Workflow Branching · Client & LOB Routing · Ownership Hierarchy · SLA / Escalation Logic · RCA · PWM Integration
Business impact12+ interconnected workflows and 1,645 configured actions support approximately 160 clients and 275 users, creating a centralized BPM operating model with improved visibility, accountability, SLA control, and RCA tracking.

HubSpot SDR Meeting Management

Sales OpsHubSpotTicketingReportingSDR Performance

A structured Sales Meeting Management pipeline in HubSpot to track SDR-generated meetings, conversion outcomes, meeting quality, cancellations, rescheduling, and SDR performance.

Business problem

Leadership needed reliable visibility into SDR-generated leads and meetings, meeting types, conversion into leads and deals, cancellation and rescheduling trends, lead quality, and SDR closure effectiveness.

  • Reporting enabled analysis of meeting volume, conversion, cancellations, rescheduling, lead quality, and SDR effectiveness.

Deal Status All-in-One: AI-Enhanced Pipeline Health

RevOpsHubSpotAIAutomationForecasting

Automates deal-health classification using CRM properties, sales activity, engagement signals, stage aging, inactivity, renewal cycles, custom code, and AI reasoning.

Business problem

Manual deal-status updates were inconsistent. Overdue, inactive, or poorly managed opportunities could remain hidden in the pipeline, creating weak forecasting visibility and unnecessary leadership focus.

Solution architecture

  • Weekly scheduled enrollment evaluates all relevant deals.
  • Deterministic rules assess close dates, inactivity, stage aging, renewal cycles, and other signals.
  • Custom code retrieves and processes deal data; AI prompts interpret contextual signals and generate a Deal Status Reason.
  • The workflow updates On Track, At Risk, Won, Lost, or Renewal status fields and feeds leadership dashboards.
TechnologyHubSpot Workflows · Custom Code · HubSpot AI Credits · AI Prompts · Deal Properties · Engagement Data · Reporting
Business impactReplaced inconsistent manual status updates with a systematic, explainable pipeline-health classification process.

Automated Product-to-Line-Item Assignment

HubSpotAutomationData QualitySales OpsNative Workflow

Automatically assigns the correct product line item to a deal based on the Provana Sub-Offering field.

Business problem

Manual product selection created avoidable errors and inconsistent deal values across roughly 40 offerings, reducing reporting and forecasting reliability.

Solution architecture

  • Deal enrollment starts when the Sub-Offering value is populated.
  • Branching maps the value to the correct product.
  • Native HubSpot line-item actions add the product to the deal.
  • An exception path notifies the team when no matching product is found.
TechnologyHubSpot Workflows · Branching · Line Items · Exception Notifications
Business impactImproves deal data quality and standardizes product assignment without external custom code.

08

NPS Survey Automation in HubSpot

Marketing OpsHubSpotFormsEmail AutomationData Hygiene

Built a repeatable NPS reminder sequence when native survey functionality at the time could not directly trigger workflows.

Business problem

The requirement was an initial survey followed by reminders only for non-responders, but the available HubSpot survey-form behavior did not support the desired workflow trigger path.

Solution architecture

  • Contacts were collected, cleaned, normalized, mapped, and segmented.
  • Normal HubSpot forms were used as a practical substitute and embedded in email templates.
  • Three emails formed the sequence: initial survey, first reminder after 7 days, second reminder after another 7 days.
  • Submission checks stop the workflow for responders while non-responders continue to the next reminder.
TechnologyHubSpot Forms · Email Templates · Segmentation · Workflows · Data Cleansing
Business impactSolved the platform limitation without custom code and created a repeatable feedback-collection framework.
09

Deal-to-Company Most Recent Billing Date Sync

HubSpotAutomationCustom CodeCRM DataAPI

Keeps the company-level latest billing date synchronized from associated won deals so account health, client status, billing/renewal reporting, and segmentation remain current.

Business problem

Billing dates were stored at deal level, while teams needed a reliable company-level date. Without synchronization, company records could become stale even when newer won deals contained updated billing information.

Solution architecture

  • Deal-based workflow enrolls when Last Billing / Client Status Date changes and the value is known.
  • A branch allows only won deals to affect the company-level field.
  • JavaScript/Node.js custom code uses HubSpot associations and API calls to identify the primary company, retrieve associated deals, calculate the latest date, and write it back.
TechnologyHubSpot Workflows · JavaScript / Node.js · HubSpot API · Private App Token · CRM Associations · Batch Processing
Business impactCreates a dependable company-level billing-date signal for reporting, health visibility, renewal logic, and segmentation.
